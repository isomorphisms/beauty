# beauty

Numerical models of human facial geometry, expression, and aging.

[Run the moving-face demonstration](demo/moving-face.html) (download and open
the HTML file), [inspect its source and tests](examples/autogenerated/moving-face/),
and [read the canonical anatomical ledger](anatomy/README.md).

The preserved 36 controls resolve to 102 independent left/right/midline muscle
actuators and a first-class mandibular/hyoid layer. A procedural connected face
now deforms through that path. Expressions combine muscle activation, rather
than selecting stored expression meshes. The model is a geometric reference;
attachments, tissue parameters and skeletal drive gains remain approximate.
